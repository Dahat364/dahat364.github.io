I am **Maocai Dai (戴茂才)**, a master's student at the [Chongqing University of Posts and Telecommunications](https://english.cqupt.edu.cn/) (CQUPT), advised by [Prof. Ye Wang](https://wangye0523.github.io/). My research focuses on **Image Aesthetic Assessment (IAA)** and **Image Quality Assessment (IQA)**. I am interested in developing visual models that better align with human perception and subjective preference.

我是重庆邮电大学硕士研究生戴茂才，导师为[王烨老师](https://wangye0523.github.io/)。我主要从事**图像美学评估（IAA）**与**图像质量评估（IQA）**研究，关注如何使视觉模型更好地对齐人类感知与主观偏好。

# 🔬 Research Interests

- **Image Aesthetic Assessment:** modeling image aesthetics through composition, style, attributes, and subjective preference.
- **Image Quality Assessment:** estimating perceptual visual quality and building robust quality representations.
- **Human-aligned Vision:** connecting computational image understanding with nuanced human visual judgments.
